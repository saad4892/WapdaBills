What the charges on your bill mean

These line items explain why your payable amount differs from units multiplied by the rate.

Fuel Price Adjustment (FPA)+−

A monthly adjustment for changes in generation fuel costs, applied per unit.

Neelum Jhelum Surcharge+−

A separate amount linked to the Neelum–Jhelum hydropower project.

Electricity duty+−

A provincial duty on consumption. The rate depends on your province and tariff.

TV fee+−

A fixed monthly fee collected with the bill where it applies.

GST / sales tax+−

Tax on the taxable parts of your bill, based on current rules.

Quarterly tariff adjustment+−

A periodic correction that reconciles earlier rates with NEPRA decisions.

Frequently asked questions

How can I check my bill without a reference number?+−

Use the consumer ID printed on your bill. If you have neither number, call the helpline or visit the subdivision office listed on an earlier bill.

What is the difference between a reference number and a consumer ID?+−

The reference number has 14 digits. The consumer ID is shorter, usually 8 to 10 digits. Either one opens your duplicate bill.

Can I pay through JazzCash or EasyPaisa?+−

Yes. Pay the amount from your duplicate bill in your wallet or bank app using the same reference number. This website does not collect payments.

When do peak hour rates apply?+−

It depends on your DISCO, meter type and season. Check the peak hours chart and confirm on your bill.

What if my bill is not updating?+−

Wait for the billing cycle to close and check the company and number. Official systems sometimes lag by a day or two.
